Tradeoff. Cells quantise to ~4% each, so the meter stops twitching on every refresh — good for something you watch all day. The reset countdown moves under each arc, buying a line back. Paint cost is 72 short strokes instead of 6, still trivial, but the cell geometry has to be precomputed once rather than derived from a single dash offset.
